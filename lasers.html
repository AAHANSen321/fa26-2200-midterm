Lasers

Laser equipment and materials are labeled RED.

  • Complete the laser cutter orientation. Find the next one on the BTU Lab Calendar.
  • Class assignments, where you need assistance, are facilitated by LAs, TAs, and instructors first, then "Lazor Wizards", if available.
  • As long as you need assistance, schedule laser appointments with a BTU Lazor Wizard. You can find them on the BTU Mischief Makers page.
  • Lazor Wizards provide "guided access". We will help you as much, or as little, as needed.
  • When you demonstrate independent and proper use of the laser cutter, you will have full access.
  • Block 1-hour times on the whiteboard next to each laser. (Not during scheduled orientations.)
